ENTITY-RELATIONSHIP MODEL 

1. ENTITIES AND ATTRIBUTES

* GYMNASIUM
  - Gym_ID (Primary Key)
  - Name (Distinguishes the gymnasium)
  - Address
  - TelephoneNumber

* MEMBER
  - UniqueIdentifier (Primary Key)
  - LastName
  - FirstName
  - Address
  - DateOfBirth
  - Gender

* SESSION
  - Session_ID (Primary Key)
  - TypeOfSport
  - Schedule
  - MaxCapacity (Fixed at a maximum of 20 members)

* COACH
  - Coach_ID (Primary Key)
  - LastName
  - FirstName
  - Age
  - Specialty


2. RELATIONSHIPS AND CARDINALITIES

* REGISTER (Between MEMBER and GYMNASIUM)
  - Description: Members can register at one of these gymnasiums.
  - Cardinality: MEMBER (1, 1) - GYMNASIUM (0, N)
  - Explanation: A member is registered at exactly one gymnasium, and a gymnasium can have many registered members.

* ATTEND (Between MEMBER and SESSION)
  - Description: Members attend training sessions, with each session accommodating a maximum of 20 members.
  - Cardinality: MEMBER (0, N) - SESSION (0, 20)
  - Explanation: A member can attend multiple sessions, and a session can accommodate anywhere from 0 up to a maximum of 20 members.

* LEAD (Between COACH and SESSION)
  - Description: Sessions are led by a maximum of two coaches.
  - Cardinality: COACH (0, N) - SESSION (1, 2)
  - Explanation: A coach can lead multiple sessions, and each session is led by 1 to 2 coaches.
